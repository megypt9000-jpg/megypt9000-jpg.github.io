ABOUT THE APP

Continue across your TVs.

TAHAWY IPTV Sync synchronizes movie and episode playback progress between TAHAWY apps on LG webOS and Samsung Tizen using your Google account. Link the same Google account, IPTV subscription and profile on each TV.

The app creates watch-progress JSON files in your Google Drive. It does not provide channels or media subscriptions. Google sign-in takes place on Google's own website. This information website does not offer Google sign-in or collect IPTV credentials.
